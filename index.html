Home Assistant Calendar

Home Assistant Calendar is a private family app. It connects the Witton household's own Google Calendar and Google Tasks to our self-hosted Home Assistant dashboard at home, so family members can see and manage upcoming events and to-do items on the household display.

Who it's for

This app is for members of the Witton household only. It isn't offered to the public, and there is no sign-up. The app runs on our own Home Assistant server at home, not on a hosted service.

What it does

  • Shows calendar events from the family's Google Calendar on the Home Assistant dashboard.
  • Lets family members add, update and complete items in their Google Tasks lists from the dashboard.

Privacy

Google data the app accesses stays on our own server and is never sold or shared. Read the full Privacy Policy.
