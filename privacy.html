Privacy Policy

Home Assistant Calendar
Effective date: 17 October 2026

Home Assistant Calendar ("the app") is a private app used only by the Witton household. It connects our own Google accounts to our self-hosted Home Assistant server at home. This policy explains what Google data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar: calendar events (titles, times, locations and descriptions) from calendars the user chooses to connect.
  • Google Tasks: task lists and the tasks in them (titles, notes, due dates and completion status).
  • Basic profile: name and email address, used only to identify which family member's account is connected.

How the data is used

The data is used only to display and manage calendar events and tasks on the family's own Home Assistant server at home, for example showing upcoming events on the household dashboard and adding or completing tasks. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to third parties. The app's operator does not transfer Google user data to anyone.

Storage and security

Data stays on our own Home Assistant server at home. The OAuth access tokens and any cached calendar or task data are stored only on that server, which is not offered as a public service. Data is fetched directly between Google's APIs and that server.

Retention and deletion

Cached data is kept only as long as needed to show it on the dashboard. Removing the integration from Home Assistant deletes its stored tokens and cached data from the server.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After you revoke access, the app can no longer read or change your Google Calendar or Google Tasks data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Home Assistant Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google user data is:

  • used only to provide the calendar and task features described above;
  • not transferred to others, except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets with notice to users;
  • not used to serve advertisements, including personalised or retargeted ads;
  • not read by humans, except with the user's affirmative consent for specific data, when needed for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or for internal operations where the data has been aggregated and anonymised.

Cookies and tracking

This website and the app do not use tracking scripts, analytics, or advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
